How it works

Four stages, in this order

Each stage unlocks the next. You always see which units are done and which still need work.

01

Pre-Test

One placement test across all six units shows your starting level on each.

36 questions · taken once
02

Learn

Reading materials are recommended for the units below target, with lessons generated per subtopic from the SKKNI reference material.

Only your weak units
03

Practice

Practice sessions draw questions from your weakest units. Every session updates your level, and you can repeat as often as you like.

Repeat until every unit is at target
04

Post-Test

Opens when all six units reach their target level, and confirms the progress you made.

Pass = all units mastered

What's inside

Everything you need between the two tests

The tools sit around the same six units, so notes, questions and discussions stay tied to what you are studying.

Reading Materials

Slides and generated lessons per subtopic, recommended from your mastery gaps.

Practice Questions

Questions matched to each unit's level, with a navigator to skip, revisit and change answers.

Data Analytics

One bar per unit shows the level reached, the target, and how far you moved since the Pre-Test.

Learning Assistant

A chat tutor available on every page for questions about the material.

Notes and Reflection

Save key points and questions as you study, then turn a session into an action plan.

Discussion

Ask and answer questions with other learners, unit by unit.
